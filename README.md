## The problem

UK small businesses aren't legally required to report their carbon footprint, but their larger clients are. Those clients are shifting contracts to suppliers who already have carbon accounting in place. Small suppliers, often with 25 to 50 staff, risk being pushed out of the supply chain as UK and EU carbon standards (CBAM) arrive.

The Hidden Footprint is a series of three books that takes the fear and mystery out of carbon accounting, so small businesses can prepare without being overcharged by consultants:

- **For owners and executives:** the effect and risk of carbon standards on the business
- **For managers:** how to build carbon accounting into existing systems and find the data
- **For accounting teams:** a practical resource for doing the work
