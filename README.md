# The Hidden Footprint

A 3-month paid consulting engagement taking [The Hidden Footprint](https://thehiddenfootprint.co.uk/), a brand-new book series, from unknown to selling. It starts with 2 weeks of discovery.

## Client goal

Help an SMB launching a new book series build product awareness, customer acquisition, and sales through AI-enabled product development and integration on its existing website.
